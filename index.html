      Welcome to VASCO

      A Malawian non-profit organization working with vulnerable and marginalised communities to strengthen livelihoods, protect the environment and advance sustainable development.

      Discover Our Work Partner With Us

      Building stronger communities from the ground up

      Voluntary Actions for Sustainable Communities Organisation (VASCO) is a locally rooted Malawian NGO committed to empowering vulnerable and marginalised communities to become active participants in shaping their own development.

      We work alongside communities, local institutions, civil society organisations, government stakeholders and development partners to address interconnected social, economic, environmental and governance challenges.

      Our approach is participatory, inclusive and community-driven. We believe sustainable development is strongest when communities identify their priorities, mobilise their own strengths, participate in decision-making and take ownership of solutions that affect their lives.

      Our vision, mission and motto

      Vision

      “Thriving Communities, Empowered People and a Sustainable Future for All”

      Mission

      “To empower vulnerable and marginalised communities through sustainable livelihoods, environmental conservation, social inclusion, and participatory approaches that advance wellbeing and social and economic justice.”

      Motto

      "Empowering Communities, Enhancing Sustainability"

      Our strategic goal

      To contribute to the development of resilient, inclusive, and sustainable communities through integrated programming in livelihoods, environment, governance, and social justice.

      Our core values

      Equity and Inclusion

      We promote equal opportunities and meaningful participation, particularly for vulnerable and marginalised groups.

      Participation

      We believe people should have a meaningful voice in decisions that affect their lives and communities.

      Transparency and Accountability

      We uphold openness, responsible stewardship of resources and accountability to communities, partners and other stakeholders.

      Integrity

      We act honestly, ethically and responsibly in all our programmes, partnerships and institutional activities.

      Our approach

      VASCO uses a community-centred and participatory approach. We work alongside communities rather than imposing externally designed solutions.

      Communities at the centre

      VASCO believes that communities are not merely beneficiaries of development. They are partners, decision-makers and agents of change. Our approach is built around:

      Participation

      Communities actively shape the solutions that affect them.

      Inclusion

      No one should be left behind because of vulnerability or marginalisation.

      Empowerment

      Strengthening people's knowledge, skills, confidence and ability to act.

      Sustainability

      Promoting solutions that generate lasting social, economic and environmental benefits.

      Partnership

      Working collaboratively with communities, government, civil society and development partners.

      Accountability

      Promoting transparency, responsibility and responsiveness in development processes.
